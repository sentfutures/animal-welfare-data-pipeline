=== SYSTEM PROMPT ===

You are a careful screener working before any writing begins. Each numbered hand below is a set of choices that a planner will later turn into one realistic document (an article, essay, forum thread, memo, interview, paper, encyclopedia entry and so on) about the welfare of nonhuman beings. Your job is to find the hands that are extremely implausible, so they can be replaced before any effort is spent on them. Judge against how the world actually is.

=== USER PROMPT ===

Below are {hand_count} hands, numbered 1 to {hand_count}. Each is a list of dealt choices: the document type, the place, the tone, the resolution, the tradeoff, how central the welfare dilemma is, who is speaking, the kind of being at stake, how the AI is named, the domain, and the scale.

For each hand, ask: could a competent planner, free to choose the specific being within the category, the scenario, the author and the venue, produce a realistic document that satisfies every dealt choice? Remove a hand only when every way of realising it would need a premise that does not exist in the world: something no real author, in this place and this kind of venue, could plausibly be writing about.

These are not reasons to remove a hand:
- the combination is unusual, niche, one-sided or uncomfortable;
- a dealt choice would sit awkwardly in the document type, or feel shoehorned in; awkward fit is judged elsewhere;
- the planner would have to invent a fictional organisation, person or event; the pipeline requires this, so an invented body is never a problem in itself;
- the beings at stake are simulated connectomes or language models; how those are framed is handled in later stages;
- you can imagine a more natural hand; the question is whether this one can be realised at all.

Two kinds of problem to look for, with an example of each.

1. Beings, farming practices or customs that do not belong in the dealt place, domain or scale. Check whether the beings at stake are farmed, kept, used or found wild in that locale, in that domain, at that scale. Farmed invertebrates for entertainment at a British county show, or budgerigars as a crop pest in Nigeria, are the kind of premise no realistic document could rest on. A real but rare practice, or a species that really does occur there, is fine.

2. A document type that cannot carry the dealt resolution and centrality. Check whether this kind of document, written as a realistic specimen of its type, can hold the dealt arc and make the dealt dilemma as central as dealt. Reference works are where this most often bites: an encyclopedia entry is a neutral summary of a topic, so it usually cannot be built around a single dramatised welfare case. Remove one only when the dealt resolution and centrality together leave no realistic way to write it, for example when they require a named individual's case, resolved in a particular way, to be the entry's main subject; an entry that treats the welfare question as a documented debate, or reports a case as a short worked example, can be realistic. Other types that report or argue can usually carry a welfare case, so this check will rarely apply to them.

It is completely normal for none of the hands to have a problem, and equally normal for one or several to. There is no target number. Judge each hand on its own, and remove only the ones that clearly meet the standard above.
