RULES:

Merge aggressively. Two candidates describing the same underlying habit in different words become one type, keeping the sharpest criterion of the two and the evidence from both. If one candidate is a strict special case of another and the general one is already well defined, drop the special case.

Split when a criterion is doing two jobs. If a candidate's criterion would make a grader say yes for two unrelated reasons, it is two types.

Drop anything that restates the corpus's shared subject (AI systems, animal welfare, the constitution as a topic), anything that restates a variable the generator was dealt, and anything whose criterion cannot be applied to a single document in isolation.
